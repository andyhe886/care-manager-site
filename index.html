How it works

One shared view for the whole family

Care Manager organizes, reminds, summarizes and lets you know when something needs attention.

1

A shared feed

Everyone sees the same log, like "Margaret marked her morning pill taken."

2

Appointments

Reminders go to the family, and you can see who is driving.

3

Medication tracking

Doses are marked taken or skipped. Missed doses show up for the family. Pill counts warn you when a refill is coming up.

4

Everyone in the loop

Brothers, sisters and Mom all see what she has chosen to share, in one place.

Her care, her choice

Mom stays in charge

Care Manager starts with Mom's permission. Nobody sees anything until she says yes.

  • Family members see nothing by default.
  • She chooses, one area at a time: medications, appointments or notes.
  • She can change her mind whenever she likes.
